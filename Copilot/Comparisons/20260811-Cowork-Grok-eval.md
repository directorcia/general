## Final Verdict

**Document B (Opus5) deserves the #1 ranking** because it demonstrates superior intellectual architecture, deeper and more disciplined evidence use, and substantially more granular financial and operational analysis. Its central insight—that per-unit pricing is the structural mechanism that turns AI-driven efficiency into customer savings rather than provider margin—is developed with greater precision and supported by stronger independent data. The explicit competing-hypothesis design and falsification criteria further raise its quality as a decision document.

**For Document A to surpass Document B it would need:**  
1. A fully specified baseline financial model (service-line P&L and three forward scenarios) comparable to Document B’s $5 m construct.  
2. Integration of independent profitability benchmarks (Service Leadership or equivalent) and academic/field AI productivity evidence.  
3. Deeper treatment of pricing mechanics and quantified historical value-migration cases.  
4. More systematic stress-testing of counter-arguments and explicit likelihood ranges or falsifiers.  

Until those gaps are closed, Document B remains the stronger research product for boards and investors who need decision-grade analysis rather than a concise briefing. Document A remains a high-quality, more accessible executive summary that could usefully serve as a shorter companion piece.
