## Head-to-Head Comparison

### Opus vs Sonnet

Effectively a tie. Opus ranks slightly higher because it appears marginally more polished and complete in execution. If the full content is substantially identical, the ranking should be treated as a statistical tie.

### Opus vs GPT56 Think

Opus proves more of its thesis and is stronger for board use. GPT56 Think is more restrained and may be more defensible under academic scrutiny, but it lacks Opus’s breadth and evidence density.

### GPT55 Quick vs Auto

GPT55 Quick is materially stronger because it is clearer, better structured, and more executive-friendly. Auto contains useful ideas but is more fragmented and less rigorous.

## Critical Findings

- The strongest reports converge on the same conclusion: the managed-services market continues growing, but the traditional labour-centric operating model is vulnerable.
- Evidence quality is the biggest differentiator. Top reports explain why evidence should be trusted; lower-ranked reports mainly repeat conclusions.
- Methodology sections materially improve credibility.
- Financial and valuation analysis separate the strongest submissions from the summaries.
- GPT55 Quick and Auto need stronger source hierarchy, counterarguments, and financial analysis.
- Auto has the most material formatting and structure issues.

## Final Verdict

**Opus deserves the top rank** because it is the most complete, best evidenced, and most board-ready submission.

To surpass it, lower-ranked documents would need deeper sourcing, clearer methodology, stronger financial and valuation analysis, better counterarguments, and more polished structure.
