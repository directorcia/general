## Final Verdict

Document B deserves the top position because it is the better editorial product: clear, evidence-disciplined, concise, and executive-ready. Document A is the deeper research product and has the strongest argument, but it needs substantial citation cleanup and compression to surpass Document B.

For Document A to overtake Document B, it should:

For Document B to widen its lead, it should:

- Expand the transformation roadmap.
- Include a slightly richer discussion of counterarguments and falsification tests.

**Bottom line:** Document B is the best overall submission. Document A is the more ambitious and substantively rich submission, but it needs editorial and citation repair before it can outrank the cleaner, more reliable Document B.
