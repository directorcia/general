## Detailed Scoring

### Document A (Opus)

| Category | Score | Commentary |
|----------|-------|------------|
| **Argument** | 9.0 | Thesis is precise and non-hyperbolic: the *category* survives; the *legacy operating model* enters selective obsolescence. Excellent logical flow from market paradox → unit economics → platform strategy → AI task exposure → scenarios → transformation ladder. Counter-arguments and falsification criteria are treated seriously (Section 12). Conclusions are tightly tethered to evidence. Complexity (bifurcation, PE roll-up risk, AI failure rates) is embraced rather than smoothed. |
| **Evidence** | 8.5 | Strong hierarchy of evidence (Tier 1 primary → Tier 2 analyst → Tier 3 vendor). Explicit vendor-proximity flags. Load-bearing claims (Gartner “half the opportunity by 2030”, Service Leadership 1.0% organic growth / 18% loss rate, Microsoft FY26 Q4 numbers, Kaseya deal-size compression) are repeatedly attributed and triangulated. Complete reference list with publication details. Minor weakness: a handful of secondary relays (Canalys via Business of Tech) and illustrative model assumptions that are clearly labelled but still model-dependent. |
| **Detail** | 8.5 | Comprehensive coverage of unit economics, Microsoft program rewiring, task-level automation exposure, historical analogies, five-year financial model, maturity ladder, and 90-day sequence. Tables are dense and purposeful. Depth is high without descending into filler. Slight over-length in places, but almost every paragraph advances the argument. |
| **Presentation** | 8.0 | Professional, board-ready tone. Clear numbered sections, effective tables, consistent British English. TOC and cross-references work. Slightly denser prose and longer paragraphs reduce skimmability compared with B. Visual hierarchy is strong but not as “executive dashboard” oriented. |

**Strengths:**

- Most rigorous treatment of counter-arguments and falsifiability.
- Clearest articulation of the bifurcation paradox and the “pricing breaks before labour” mechanism.
- Best methodological transparency (four parallel streams, evidence tiers, limitations).
- Highest-quality integration of Microsoft FY26 Q4 data (released the day of finalisation).
- Strongest scenario weighting and owner-fate estimates with explicit caveats.

**Weaknesses:**

- Density can impede rapid executive consumption.
- A few market-size figures rely on secondary relays.
- Illustrative five-year model, while well-anchored, remains illustrative and could be stress-tested more explicitly.
- Reference list is exhaustive but occasionally mixes primary and secondary citations without perfect separation.

**Distinguishing elements:**

Explicit evidence hierarchy, falsification section, and the single most important citation call-out (Gartner Oct 2025) give it an edge in intellectual honesty and decision-usefulness for a sceptical owner.
